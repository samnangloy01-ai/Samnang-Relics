28 Essential Ancient Cambodian Temples Beyond Angkor Wat

Angkor Wat shines globally, but exploring beyond reveals stunning ancient Khmer architecture, hidden ruins, and centuries of rich history.

Published: September 17, 2026 • Written by Urban Explorer 4K

The Hindu-Buddhist Khmer Empire stretched across what is now known as Cambodia, leaving behind numerous majestic relics. While Angkor Wat remains the most famous, venturing into remote provinces unveils spectacular historic structures.

IMG Code: 01000713

ប្រាសាទប្រាំ (ព្រំប្រទល់ខេត្តសៀមរាប-ព្រះវិហារ)

Century: 10th Century

Located on the remote border region between Siem Reap and Preah Vihear, featuring classic architectural elements of the period.

IMG Code: 01000787

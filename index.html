About the Creator

Welcome! I'm Samnang, an explorer and digital content creator driven by a deep passion for historic architecture, ancient stonework, and forgotten ruins. My mission is to travel across world heritage sites—from ancient Cambodian temples to global historical monuments—capturing their architectural grandeur and preserving their rich histories through high-quality video and photographic documentation.

Featured Architecture & History (Ancient Temples)

1. ប្រាសាទពងទឹក (Pong Toek Temple)

Location: Cambodia

Exploring the quiet woodland setting of Pong Toek Temple, featuring laterite and sandstone construction, ancient carved lintels, and false doors nestled within nature, capturing the timeless essence of Cambodia's hidden heritage.

2. វត្តពោធិ៍ដំណាក់ (Po Damnak Temple)

Location: Cambodia

Documenting the cultural and historical significance of Po Damnak Temple, showcasing traditional architecture and spiritual heritage preserved through generations.

3. ប្រាសាទភ្នំសណ្តុក (Phnom Sandok Temple)

Location: Cambodia

Venturing up the ancient hilltop sanctuary of Phnom Sandok, capturing panoramic views of the surrounding landscape, magnificent stone ruins, and historical Khmer architecture nestled amidst nature.

Visual Chronicle: Life on the Water

Location: Tonle Sap Lake, Cambodia

Venturing deep into the heart of Cambodia's aquatic communities to capture the raw, beautiful rhythm of daily life. From traditional stilted wooden houses and floating structures to calm waterways framed by lush greenery, every corner tells a unique story of culture and resilience.

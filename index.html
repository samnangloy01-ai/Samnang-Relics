Featured Architecture & History

Angkor Wat & Khmer Monuments

Location: Siem Reap, Cambodia

Constructed during the early 12th century under King Suryavarman II, Angkor Wat stands as the largest religious monument in the world. Originally dedicated to the Hindu deity Vishnu, it represents the pinnacle of classical Khmer architecture, featuring intricate bas-reliefs, soaring towers, and complex hydraulic engineering.

Visual Chronicle: Life on the Water

Location: Tonle Sap Lake, Cambodia

Venturing deep into the heart of Cambodia's aquatic communities to capture the raw, beautiful rhythm of daily life. From traditional stilted wooden houses and floating structures to calm waterways framed by lush greenery, every corner tells a unique story of culture and resilience.

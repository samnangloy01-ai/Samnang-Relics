28 Essential Ancient Cambodian Temples Beyond Angkor Wat

Angkor Wat shines globally, but exploring beyond reveals stunning ancient Khmer architecture, hidden ruins, and centuries of rich history.

1. Prasat Pram (Border Siem Reap-Preah Vihear)

ប្រាសាទប្រាំ (ព្រំប្រទល់ខេត្តសៀមរាប-ព្រះវិហារ)

Century: 10th Century

Located on the remote border region between Siem Reap and Preah Vihear, featuring classic architectural elements of the period.

2. Prasat Pram (Koh Ker)

ប្រាសាទប្រាំ (តំបន់កោះកេរ)

Century: 10th Century

A remarkable brick temple ruin located within the majestic archaeological complex of Koh Ker.

3. Preah Khan of Kampong Svay

ប្រាសាទព្រះខ័នកំពង់ស្វាយ

Century: 12th Century

One of the largest provincial temple complexes of the Khmer Empire, hidden deep within forested lands.

4. Pong Toek Temple

ប្រាសាទពងទឹក

Century: 10th-11th Century

An ancient sanctuary site reflecting early architectural traditions.

5. Po Damnak Temple

ប្រាសាទពោធិ៍ដំណាក់

Century: 10th-11th Century

A historical site surrounded by nature, holding rich archaeological significance.

6. Trapeang Prasat Temple

ប្រាសាទត្រពាំងប្រាសាទ

Century: 9th-10th Century

Ancient structured brick and stone remains dating back to the earlier periods of Khmer development.

7. Trapeang Prasat Toch Temple

ប្រាសាទត្រពាំងប្រាសាទតូច

Century: 12th Century (Hospital / Rest House)

Believed to have served as an ancient hospital or traveler's rest house along historic routes.

8. Prasat Toch

ប្រាសាទតូច

Century: 12th Century (Hospital / Rest House)

Another classic example of Jayavarman VII's social structure architecture (Chapel/Hospital).

9. Preah Vihear Temple

ប្រាសាទព្រះវិហារ

Century: Historic Mountain Architecture

An architectural masterpiece perched on a natural cliff of the Dângrêk Mountains.

10. Cha Eh Temple

ប្រាសាទចាអេះ

Century: 11th Century

An old jungle ruin showcasing traditional 11th-century craftsmanship.

11. Suor Mab Temple

ប្រាសាទសួរប៉ាប់

Century: 10th Century

An ancient sanctuary structure hidden in historical landscapes.

12. Kuk Pros Temple

ប្រាសាទគុកប្រុស

Century: 10th Century

Part of twin style structures traditionally tied to local folklore.

13. Kuk Srei Temple

ប្រាសាទគុកស្រី

Century: 10th Century

Complementary historical structure associated with the regional temple network.

14. Neak Buos Temple

ប្រាសាទនាគបួស

Century: 10th-11th Century

A fascinating mountaintop or remote forest sanctuary with unique layouts.

15. Seima Temple

ប្រាសាទសីមា

Century: 11th Century

Known for its boundary markers and classic structural elements.

16. South Sneang Krabei Temple

ប្រាសាទស្នែងក្របីត្បូង

Century: 10th Century

Southern tower ruin of the Snaeng Krabei group.

17. North Snaeng Krabei Temple

ប្រាសាទស្នែងក្របីជើង

Century: 11th Century

Northern sanctuary showing unique stone masonry and tree integration.

18. Prasat O Krouch Temple

ប្រាសាទអូរក្រូច

Century: 11th Century

A quiet, secluded ruin surrounded by old trees and nature.

19. Prasat Tram Preal

ប្រាសាទត្រាំប្រាល

Century: 10th Century

An ancient temple site holding deep historical remnants.

20. Lom Neang Temple

ប្រាសាទលោមនាង

Century: 10th Century

Characterized by beautiful brickwork intertwined with large tree roots.

21. Chau Kom Mon Temple

ប្រាសាទចៅកំម៉ុន

Century: 11th Century

A secluded sanctuary site tucked away in forest canopies.

22. Punnareay Temple

ប្រាសាទពណ្ណរាយ

Century: 12th Century

An architectural structure built during the peak empire era.

23. Khnar Temple

ប្រាសាទខ្នារ

Century: 10th-11th Century

Features intricate lintels and historical stone carvings.

24. Chaeng Maeng Temple

ប្រាសាទចែងម៉ែង

Century: 12th Century

A historic ruin exhibiting laterite blocks and classic styling.

25. Kampheum Temple

ប្រាសាទកំភើម

Century: 7th Century (Chenla Period)

An ancient pre-Angkorian ruin dating back to the Chenla era.

26. Prasat Preah Lean

ប្រាសាទព្រះលាន

Century: 12th Century

A remarkable site with collapsed structures and stone terraces.

27. Krapum Chhouk Temple

ប្រាសាទក្រពុំឈូក

Century: 13th Century

A late-period architectural monument representing post-Angkor transition.

28. Khnar Phtol Temple

ប្រាសាទខ្នារផ្ទុល

Century: 11th Century

The final majestic site in our catalog, showcasing classic 11th-century temple design.

Intricate Bas-Relief Carvings

ចម្លាក់ផ្ទាំងថ្មបុរាណ

Description: Ancient Wall Carvings

A stunning ancient wall featuring rows of intricate seated figures carved directly into the sandstone blocks.

Featured Architecture & History

Angkor Wat & Khmer Monuments

Location: Siem Reap, Cambodia

Historical Significance: Constructed during the early 12th century under King Suryavarman II, Angkor Wat stands as the largest religious monument in the world. Originally dedicated to the Hindu deity Vishnu, it represents the pinnacle of classical Khmer architecture, featuring intricate bas-reliefs, soaring towers, and complex hydraulic engineering.
